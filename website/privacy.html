Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

Fit Check ("we", "us") is a social app for building a digital closet, getting outfit ideas and feedback, and sharing outfits. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

Who can use Fit Check

Fit Check is only for people aged 18 and over. We ask for your date of birth when you sign up to confirm this. We use it for that check only. We do not store your date of birth. We only record that you confirmed you are 18 or older.

What we collect

  • Account details: email address, password (stored securely hashed by our provider), username, display name, optional bio and profile photo.
  • Your closet: photos of clothing you add, plus the details you give them (type, colour, formality, warmth, notes). Your closet is private to you.
  • Posts and activity: outfit photos you post, captions, the occasion, ratings and feedback you choose to publish, people you tag, likes, comments, star ratings, follows, blocks and reports.
  • Saved outfits: outfit suggestions you choose to save.
  • Technical data: basic logs our hosting provider keeps to run and secure the service (for example IP address and request times).

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, or advertising ID, and we do not show ads.

How the AI features work

Outfit ideas and outfit ratings run on your phone, using Google's on-device Gemini Nano model (through Android's ML Kit GenAI APIs and AICore). The photos and text you give the AI are processed on your device. They are not sent to us or to an AI company for these features.

Google may collect limited metrics about how the on-device AI features perform (for example, whether a request succeeded and how long it took), as described in Google's ML Kit terms. On phones without on-device AI, Fit Check uses built-in style rules instead, which also run on your phone.

AI ratings are only about clothing and styling. They are not a judgement of your body or appearance, and they can be wrong.

How we use your information

  • To run your account and the app's features (closet, outfit ideas, posting, feed, comments, ratings, follows, notifications).
  • To keep the community safe: reviewing reports, removing content that breaks our Terms, and preventing abuse.
  • To comply with the law and respond to lawful requests.

Who can see what

  • Only you: your closet, saved outfits, blocks, reports you make, and your email address.
  • Other signed-in Fit Check users: your username, display name, bio, profile photo, posts, comments, likes, ratings you give and receive, and who you follow. People you block cannot see your posts or comments.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information or share it for advertising. We use these service providers to run Fit Check:

  • Supabase hosts our database, sign-in and photo storage (servers in Ireland, EU).
  • Google provides the on-device AI (ML Kit / Gemini Nano) and distributes the app through Google Play.

We may disclose information if required by law, or to report child sexual abuse material to the relevant authorities (see our Child Safety Standards).

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is active. When you delete your account, we delete your profile, closet, photos, posts, comments, likes, ratings, follows and notifications straight away. Reports you made are kept without your identity so we can finish any safety review. Backups held by our provider are overwritten within their standard backup cycle.

Your choices and rights

  • Edit your profile, or delete any post, comment or closet item, at any time in the app.
  • Delete your account in the app under Profile → Settings → Delete account, or request deletion here.
  • Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to access, correct, export or object to the processing of your data, and to complain to your data protection authority. Contact us to use these rights.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Database access rules make sure people can only read and change what they are allowed to. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

Changes

If we make important changes to this policy, we will tell you in the app before they take effect.
