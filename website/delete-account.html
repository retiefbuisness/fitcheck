Delete your Fit Check account

Fit Check account and data deletion

Option 1: in the app (instant)

  1. Open Fit Check and go to your Profile.
  2. Tap Settings (the gear icon).
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account is deleted immediately.

Option 2: by email

If you can't use the app, email retiefbuisness@gmail.com from the email address on your account, with the subject "Delete my account" and your username. We will delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile (username, display name, bio, profile photo) and sign-in details
  • Your closet items and their photos, and your saved outfits
  • Your posts and their photos, comments, likes, star ratings, tags, follows, blocks and notifications

What we keep

  • Reports you submitted about other content are kept without your identity so safety reviews can be finished.
  • Copies in our hosting provider's backups are overwritten within their standard backup cycle.
  • Information we must keep by law, such as material reported to authorities for child safety, is kept only as long as the law requires.
