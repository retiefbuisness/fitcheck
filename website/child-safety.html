Child Safety Standards

Last updated: 29 September 2026

Fit Check has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming, sextortion, and any content that sexualises or endangers minors.

Our standards

  • Fit Check is for adults aged 18+ only. Sign-up requires a date of birth check, and accounts we believe belong to minors are removed.
  • Users may not post images of anyone under 18.
  • Any content or account involved in CSAE is removed and permanently banned.
  • We report apparent CSAM to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and/or the relevant national authorities, as required by law, and cooperate with law enforcement.

How to report

  • In the app: tap ⋯ on any post, comment or profile → Report → "Child safety concern". The content is hidden from everyone immediately while we review it.
  • By email: retiefbuisness@gmail.com
  • If a child is in immediate danger, contact your local police first.

Child safety contact

Our designated contact for child safety matters, including requests from Google Play and law enforcement: the Fit Check safety team, retiefbuisness@gmail.com.
